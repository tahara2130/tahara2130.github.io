Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot ("the Bot") handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

At this time, the Bot does not collect, store, or process any personal information from its users.

The Bot does not maintain a database or any other persistent storage containing information about Discord users, servers, messages, or interactions.

2. Discord Information

The Bot may technically receive information from Discord while operating, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message content, or other information provided through Discord's API.

However, the Bot does not currently store or retain this information.

The Bot only has access to the information required by Discord to deliver its functionality and operate as a Discord application.

3. Data Storage

The Bot does not currently store user data, server data, message history, or other personal information.

No user profiles, tracking databases, analytics databases, or permanent logs containing personal information are maintained by the Bot.

4. Third-Party Services

The Bot does not currently use third-party services to collect, store, or process user information.

Discord itself remains subject to its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service .

5. Data Retention

Since the Bot does not currently store personal information, there is no personal data retention period.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Bot's functionality may change in the future. If the Bot begins collecting, processing, or storing personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is stored.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the Bot's handling of information, you may contact the Bot's developer through the support or contact method provided with the Bot.
